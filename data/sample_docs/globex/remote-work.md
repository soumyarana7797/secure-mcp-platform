# Globex Remote Work Guidelines

Globex employees may work remotely up to three days per week with the agreement of their team lead. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone, when everyone is expected to be reachable.

Employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 500 euros for a desk, chair or monitor. Internet costs are reimbursed up to 40 euros per month. Company laptops must use the managed VPN when accessing internal systems from home.

Fully remote arrangements need approval from the department director and are reviewed every six months.
