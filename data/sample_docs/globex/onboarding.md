# Globex New Hire Onboarding

On the first day new hires receive their laptop, badge and accounts. IT sets up single sign-on and multi-factor authentication before the end of day one. Each new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy for the first 90 days.

During the first week you complete mandatory training on data protection, the code of conduct and the information security basics. Your manager agrees a 30, 60 and 90 day plan with you in the first week.

Probation lasts six months. Feedback conversations take place at the end of month one, three and six.
