# Acme Security Incident Runbook

If you suspect a security incident, such as a lost laptop, a phishing email you clicked, or unauthorised access to a system, report it immediately to the security team through the incident hotline or the security channel. Do not try to investigate on your own.

The on-call security engineer acknowledges the report within 15 minutes and opens an incident ticket. Affected accounts are locked and credentials rotated. If customer data may be involved, the legal team must be notified within 24 hours to meet breach notification rules.

After resolution the team writes a blameless post-incident review within five working days and shares the lessons learned.
