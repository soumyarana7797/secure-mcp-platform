# Acme Vacation Policy

Full-time employees at Acme accrue 1.5 days of paid vacation per month, which is 18 days per year. Unused vacation days carry over into the next calendar year up to a maximum of 10 days. Days above that limit expire on 31 March.

Vacation requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal. Your manager approves or declines the request within three working days. Requests longer than ten consecutive days need approval from a department head.

During the December holiday shutdown the office is closed between 25 December and 1 January. These days do not count against your vacation balance.
