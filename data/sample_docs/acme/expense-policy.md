# Acme Expense Reimbursement

Employees may claim reasonable business expenses such as travel, accommodation and client meals. Submit every claim in the finance portal within 30 days, attaching the original receipt. Claims without receipts are rejected.

Flights must be booked in economy class for trips under six hours. Hotel stays are capped at 180 dollars per night in major cities and 130 dollars elsewhere. Client meals are capped at 60 dollars per person.

Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll. Expenses above 1,000 dollars need written pre-approval from your manager and the finance team.
